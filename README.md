# MDD Cold-Start Risk Audit

# 《Auditing Selective Prediction under Information Budgets at Cold-Start Sites in Resting-State fMRI of Major Depressive Disorder》

MDD Cold-Start Risk Audit investigates the feasibility of reliable selective prediction and statistical risk certification for major depressive disorder (MDD) classification at previously unseen fMRI sites. The project provides a systematic evaluation under three information-budget regimes: source-only transfer (S0), unlabeled-target weighting (S1), and limited-label target-local certification (S2). Using 2,232 rs-fMRI acquisitions from 23 REST-meta-MDD sites, it examines cross-site selective risk, confidence-based abstention, and the sample-size constraints that limit formal risk certification.

This project accompanies our manuscript prepared for submission to ISBI 2027.
